# RustSec advisories accepted for engine/Cargo.lock (CONTRIBUTING.md,
# "Dependency advisories"). `cargo audit` skips the ids listed here, in
# the release build and in the weekly audit (packaging/audit-ignore.sh).
#
# One advisory per line, three fields separated by blanks:
#
#   RUSTSEC-YYYY-NNNN  YYYY-MM-DD  why the advisory does not affect Seldon
#
# The date is the expiry, at most one year ahead. From that day on the
# entry fails the release build until it is reviewed again (new date) or
# removed. Entries are reviewed like code. Blank lines and lines starting
# with `#` are ignored.
