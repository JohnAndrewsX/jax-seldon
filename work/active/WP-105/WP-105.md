# WP-105 — Redact collector messages before cursors.json, capture --json and the index

Role: Engine Dev (Opus, high). 0.1.5. From the WP-099 reviews (info
finding; stage 2 follow-up 2). Touches §7 and §8 (the plugin renders
`state.collectors.message`).

## Goal

A collector's message (e.g. snapper's stderr on a failure) is redacted
everywhere it is written or shown, not only in the ledger copy: in
`cursors.json`, in `capture --json` and in `index.json`
`state.collectors[].message`.

## Inputs

engine/src/redact.rs (the redactor), engine/src/commands/capture.rs
(where collector states are saved and printed), engine/src/index/ (state
collectors), the WP-099 handover (Decision 2) and review packets,
docs/SPEC-ENGINE.md §7 (the sentence WP-099 adds about where messages
are and are not redacted).

## Outputs

- One redaction pass on a collector message before it is stored or
  printed; tests with a planted token in a stub collector's stderr: the
  token is masked in cursors.json, capture --json and index.json; the
  ledger copy unchanged; idempotent.
- SPEC §7 sentence updated; CHANGELOG (neutral). No contract change
  (`message` stays a string).

## Acceptance

`just check` 0; mutants. Review: stage 1 Opus, stage 2 Fable (privacy).
