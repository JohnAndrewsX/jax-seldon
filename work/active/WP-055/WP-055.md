## Goal

`seldon hook session-start` lives in its own module and its tests in
their own file, with **no behaviour change**: the output stays
byte-identical.

## Inputs

engine/src/commands/hook.rs (`session_start` ~874, `status_summary` ~931,
`case_block` ~953 and the unit tests in `mod tests` that call them),
engine/src/commands/agent.rs:222 (`hook::session_start`),

engine/tests/golden/session-start.txt.

## Outputs

- `engine/src/commands/hook/context.rs` (new) holds `session_start`,
  `status_summary`, `case_block` and their unit tests, moved unchanged;
  `hook.rs` gets `mod context;` and `pub use context::session_start;`, so
  `hook::session_start` keeps resolving. Shared helpers stay in `hook.rs`
  as `pub(super)`.
- `engine/tests/session_context.rs` (new) holds the three session-start
  integration tests, moved unchanged. A helper they need that lives only
  in `tests/hooks.rs` (e.g. the `Hooks` struct) is copied, not moved.
- Nothing else.
