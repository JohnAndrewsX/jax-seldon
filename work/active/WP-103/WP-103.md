# WP-103 — Config replay with redacted twins; SPEC §7 clause

Role: Engine Dev (Opus, high). After WP-093 (merged). From the WP-093
reviews (stage 1 and 2). Small.

## Goal

Two watched files whose names differ only in a part the redaction masks
(e.g. two desktop entries with different e-mail local parts) no longer
collide when the config collector replays a generation after a failed
cursor save.

## Inputs

engine/src/collectors/config.rs (`replay`: a `BTreeMap` keyed by the
redacted subject, ≈ line 822), the WP-093 handover (Decisions 2), the
WP-093 review packets (private folder), docs/SPEC-ENGINE.md §7 (the
`:` rule of the `email` rule, ≈ lines 1042–1046).

## Outputs

- `replay` maps a redacted subject to every path with that subject and
  chooses by `hashFrom` (or another unambiguous key); a test with two
  twins: add, change, remove each, a failed cursor save in between,
  replay records each change once.
- SPEC §7: the "character other than white space" clause of the email
  rule gains "(also a marker another rule left there)".
- CHANGELOG line if behaviour changes.

## Acceptance

`just check` 0; mutants listed. Review: stage 1 Opus.
