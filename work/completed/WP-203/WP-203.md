# WP-203 — every listed drift item keeps its event in the index

0.2.x. From the WP-200 Fable review (2026-10-11, follow-ups a and b;
ADR-0053 consequences). Two gaps that exist in 0.2.0 without any cut:

- (a) The build lists the newest 500 events (CONTRACT.md rule 4). A
  drift item whose leader event is older than those 500 is listed in
  `drift[]` but has no Changelog row: on a ×150 logbook 0 of 200 crisis
  rows had an event. The chips, NEEDS YOU, the triage view and the sheet
  lose the row while the pill counts it.
- (b) `index::fit` (ADR-0053) may cut a drift row that an unapplied
  triage proposal names; the item then reads "No longer open" although
  `drift apply` still decides it by the engine's own classification.

Role: Engine Dev (Opus, high). Reviewer: Opus, then Fable (contract).

**Do.** (a) The build lists the leader event of every listed drift row,
past the 500 newest (at most 200 extra events, rule 4's drift cap);
measure the size on the ×150 fixture. (b) `fit` keeps the drift rows an
unapplied proposal names, as it keeps listed crisis rows, if
`triage::read` can name them without a new field; otherwise say why
not and leave (b) to an ADR. Update CONTRACT.md rule 4's wording
("newest 500, plus the event of every listed drift item"), the
reference derive in `scripts/validate-fixtures.py`, the golden
fixtures, SPEC-ENGINE §6 and ADR-0053's consequences only by a new ADR
row or an amendment the reviewer accepts (ADR-0053 is accepted and
immutable). No `contractVersion` bump: a 0.2.0 plugin reads the extra
events as ordinary rows (prove it with the 0.2.0 `Model.js`).

**Acceptance.** A test with a crisis row whose event is older than the
500 newest shows the event in `events[]` and a Changelog row in the
0.2.0 model; the budget tests of WP-200 still pass; `just check` green
with `SELDON_FULL_CHECK=1`; evidence words in the handover.

**Inputs.** CONTRACT.md rules 4–5; ADR-0053; `engine/src/index/build.rs`,
`engine/src/index/budget.rs`, `engine/src/index/triage.rs`;
`scripts/validate-fixtures.py`; `plugin/Model.js` (`changelogRows`,
`driftItemFor`, `triageView`); private
`review-0.1.1/handovers/WP-200-stage2-fable.md` (joins 1 and 3).

Depends on: WP-200 merged into `next`.
