# WP-139 — Recently edited under ~/.config, outside the watched paths

Ships in 0.2.0 on the integration branch `next` (branch from and merge into `next`). Operator decision 2026-10-07 after the comparison with the Omarchy plugin "What changed" (nejcc.what-changed, MIT). The idea is taken, not its code: write our own.

Role: Engine Dev with a plugin part (Opus, high). Small to medium. Order: W1, last.

**Goal.** Seldon's watched paths are exact (hash, ledger) but narrow (12
defaults). Edits under `~/.config` outside them, such as alacritty,
`git/config` or `starship.toml`, are invisible today. Show them as an
informational list in the desk's System section: path, time, "not
watched". It never writes to the ledger. One click adds the path to
`watchPaths`.

**Inputs.**
- SPEC-ENGINE §2 and §4 (watchPaths, skipPaths, redaction).
- The config collector.
- ADR-0028, if a newly watched path gets a class.
- The desk's System section (WP-123).
- WP-138 (shares the scan).

**Outputs.**
- A bounded mtime scan of `~/.config` during capture:
  - newest 80, 7 days;
  - our own ignore list: caches, browser and Electron profiles (any folder
    with `Cookies` or `Local State`), state, log, lock, databases,
    images, `.git`, the plugin folders, `shell.json`;
  - `[redaction] skipPaths` honoured.
- The result goes into the index as an optional list (ADR-0038 amendment)
  or into `seldon preview`'s shape. It holds paths only, never content.
- "Watch this path" runs a fixed-argv config command, with no shell
  string.
- Tests: the ignore list, the bounds, skipPaths, and the click writing
  `watchPaths`.

**Acceptance.**
- `just check` is green.
- The capture-cost delta is measured.
- No path under a skipPath appears.
