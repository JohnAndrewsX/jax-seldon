# WP-169 — Terminal escapes and hidden text in what the engine writes

Ships in 0.2.x. From the WP-159 and WP-166 reviews. Security; stage 2
Fable.

Role: Engine Dev (Opus, high). Small to medium.

**Do.**
- ANSI CSI/OSC sequences are removed as whole units (not just the ESC)
  from the copy redaction reads, so `\e[01;31m\e[Kpassword\e[m\e[K=…`
  (grep colour output) is masked; crash logs pasted into `inbox add`
  (WP-166) are the main case.
- Decide whether raw ESC may stay in the journal and logbook Markdown at
  all (a terminal-injection channel when someone `cat`s them); if not,
  every writer drops or escapes it (one helper).
- Tag characters (U+E0000–E007F) that spell text ("ASCII smuggling") in
  notes that agents read: mark or drop them (WP-159 review N3).
- The Cf gaps of WP-159 review N2 (U+06DD, 070F, 0890–0891, 08E2, 110BD,
  110CD, 13430–1343F) as Fable judges.

- From WP-159's Fable stage 2: U+0085 (NEL) stays in the redaction copy
  but terminals draw nothing for it, so `to<U+0085>ken=` is not masked —
  drop it from the copy too. For the Cf gaps: widen only the redaction
  copy's `hides_from_rules` with `\p{Cf}` (regex tables, no new crate);
  the shared `is_invisible` set stays. U+06DD, 070F, 0890–0891, 08E2,
  110BD and 110CD carry glyphs: no change.

**Inputs** (from WP-166 round 3c, Fable stage 2).
- `seldon inbox add`'s two passes (`engine/src/commands/inbox.rs`,
  SPEC-ENGINE §3): the text is redacted as given, then its control
  characters are dropped (all but tab and newline; in the title, all but
  the line ends), then it is redacted again with the invisible
  characters dropped after it. The rule: nothing is removed after the
  last redaction that its reading copy kept. The ADR of this WP should
  state that order as part of ADR-0048's model.
- Its residual: two lone controls in mixed roles in one secret
  (`done<CR>sk<CR>-…`, also BS+BS, BS+CR) are filed glued
  (`donesk-…`), as two invisible characters are in `import task` and
  `log` (`done<ZW>sk<ZW>-…`). It is the limit of ADR-0048 §2's two
  readings; closing it would need a reading that both deletes and bounds
  at each control.
- An ESC CSI sequence inside a key (`sk-ABCDEFGHIJ<ESC>[0mKLMN…`)
  leaves the fragments interleaved with `[0m` (the CSI/OSC item above).

**Acceptance.** Tests per case; redaction bench within budget; `just
check` green.

## From the prototype debate (2026-10-09)

Source: `jax-seldon-private/prototype-0.3/debate-2026-10-09/SYNTHESIS.md`;
operator decisions E48–E63 (STATUS.md, all as recommended).

- **Scope widened:** the escape and hidden-text rules also cover report
  drafts (WP-186) and crash analyses filed through `inbox add` (WP-166,
  used by WP-185). This WP lands before WP-185 and WP-186.
- **Threat model (E61):** the crash inbox guards against mistakes (a
  pasted log with colour codes, a stray control), not an adversary who
  crafts input. The mixed-role residual above (two lone controls in mixed
  roles glued, `done<CR>sk<CR>-…` → `donesk-…`, likewise BS+BS, BS+CR and
  two invisible characters in `import task` and `log`) is accepted under
  that model: the ADR records it as a residual; no reading that both
  deletes and bounds at each control is required.
