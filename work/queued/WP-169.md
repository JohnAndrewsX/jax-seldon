# WP-169 — Terminal escapes and hidden text in what the engine writes

Ships in 0.2.x. From the WP-159 and WP-166 reviews. Security; stage 2
Fable.

Role: Engine Dev (Opus, high). Small to medium.

**Do.**
- ANSI CSI/OSC sequences are removed as whole units (not just the ESC)
  from the copy redaction reads, so `\e[01;31m\e[Kpassword\e[m\e[K=…`
  (grep colour output) is masked; crash logs pasted into `inbox add`
  (WP-166) are the main case.
- Decide whether raw ESC may stay in the journal and logbook Markdown at
  all (a terminal-injection channel when someone `cat`s them); if not,
  every writer drops or escapes it (one helper).
- Tag characters (U+E0000–E007F) that spell text ("ASCII smuggling") in
  notes that agents read: mark or drop them (WP-159 review N3).
- The Cf gaps of WP-159 review N2 (U+06DD, 070F, 0890–0891, 08E2, 110BD,
  110CD, 13430–1343F) as Fable judges.

**Acceptance.** Tests per case; redaction bench within budget; `just
check` green.
