Role: Engine Dev (Opus). Later (0.1.5 or on the operator's word). After
WP-109 and one real Omarchy update on the test host.

watched paths and what stays attention after an update; decide further
`routinePaths` defaults or evidence rules (e.g. a git-cloned theme as
`theme-repo`); propose the contract v2 fields (`events[].routine`,
`drift[].class`, `summary.attention`).

host's ledger after the update; `$OMARCHY_PATH/bin`, migrations.

rule; ADR amendment or new ADR for contract v2 fields.
