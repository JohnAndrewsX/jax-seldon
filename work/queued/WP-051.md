round-2/). Independent of WP-049.

## Goal

The repository and the plugin use the Seldon mark instead of the `⟡`
fallback glyph and the placeholder preview; every surface the brief
lists has its asset.

## Inputs

docs/DESIGN-BRIEF.md (+§6a), work/design/ROUND-2.md, ROUND-3.md,
work/design/round-2/{README,DELIVERY}.md and files; docs/SPEC-PLUGIN.md

## Outputs

1. `assets/` with the round-3 files, `assets/README.md`, `DELIVERY.md`,

   the record.

   the matching empty/status states at 48/96; A12 markers in the chart

   brackets, crisis spindle). Images live under `plugin/assets/` (split
   repo rule, ADR-0009).

   docs site would use it (`assets/` only for now).

   1.25, glyph centre on the digit centre within 1 px; screenshots in
   `work/design/round-3/` and the result line appended to
   `work/design/ROUND-3.md`.
5. SPEC-PLUGIN §4–§6 updated (glyph, header mark, pictograms, marker
   shapes); `plugin/preview.png` re-rendered; CHANGELOG entry.

## Acceptance
