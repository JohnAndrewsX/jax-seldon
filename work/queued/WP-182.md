# WP-182 — Desk header, forms and pill marks: the operator's ideas

0.2.y (ADR amendment and a CONTRACT row, no `contractVersion` bump). From
the prototype 0.3 debate (`jax-seldon-private/prototype-0.3/debate-2026-10-09/`,
SYNTHESIS.md §3 NEW-7; items A11(a)(b)(d), B8); operator decisions E53
(pill `A · !C`, no ⏸), E42 (command row without a bump).

Role: Engine + Plugin Dev (Opus, high). Small engine, medium plugin.
Reviewer: Opus; Fable stage 2 (engine write path, contract row). ADR:
proposed by this WP (Fable writes, next free ADR number): "Pill marks and
desk header" — amends ADR-0028 §4a (`A · !C`), ADR-0034 §2 (Capture
writable through the one settings path) and ADR-0034 §7 (the pill's
look). The operator accepts the text (E53 gave the direction).

**Do.**
- **A11(a) New case and link.** Engine: `seldon plan new … --link
  <eventId> [--only] --json` creates the case and links the event in one
  write under one lock (the two-command chain `plan new` + `drift link`
  leaves an orphan case when the second fails). CONTRACT.md row + fixture
  (E42). Plugin: the link box (`components/desk/DriftForm.qml`) gets
  *Existing case / New case*; the existing-case picker is a themed list
  (filter, the proposed case first), not a native dropdown.
- **A11(b) Capture interval.** Settings › Capture: six preset chips
  5 · 10 · 15 · 30 · 60 · 120 min and *Custom* = a step-5 field 5–120;
  the manifest's `captureIntervalMin` schema stays (min 5, max 120,
  step 5). Written through Omarchy's plugin settings once per pick, never
  per keystroke.
- **A11(d) Capture now.** *Capture now* at the right of the header with a
  still ring stepped per minute from a `nextCaptureMs` that
  `plugin/Service.qml` tracks (timer fire, interval change, manual
  capture). Tooltip "Next capture in about N min" — never a clock time
  (the Timer exposes none; suspend shifts it, SYNTHESIS §7.16). No pulse.
  No ring while the Timer is off.
- **B8 Pill marks (E53).** `Model.pillText`: the `!` is attached to the
  **crisis count** only — `2 · !1` under `driftInBar = crisis`,
  `2 · 7 !1` under `all` (`!D` must not read as D crises). **No ⏸**:
  stale stays dimmed, its tooltip leads "Not up to date: last capture
  3 h ago". Vertical bars unchanged. The pill is byte-identical in EASY
  and PRO.
- SPEC-PLUGIN §4 and §5 (header, Settings), docs/CONTRACT.md, CHANGELOG.

**Acceptance.** `--link` is idempotent on retry and tested on the
lock-held path (exit 4, nothing written); the chips write once per pick;
the ring is never shown while the Timer is off; `pillText` cases incl.
`all`, `none` and stale; `bar-view.sh` alignment unchanged; `seldon view
pill` identical in both modes; `just check` green (private HOME and
XDG_RUNTIME_DIR).
