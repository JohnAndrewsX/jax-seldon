# WP-052 — `init --theme-hook` writes under the lock

Role: Engine Dev (Opus, high). Small follow-up from the WP-049 review
(round 2, open question 2). Independent of everything else.

## Goal

`seldon init --theme-hook` installs the theme-set hook script and
records it as an own write (WP-038) the way `hook install` does since
WP-049: lock taken before the write, held through the record and the
commit, exit 4 with nothing written while another `seldon` holds it.

## Inputs

engine/src/commands/{init,setup,hook}.rs (the WP-049 lock pattern in
hook.rs is the template), docs/SPEC-ENGINE.md §5 rule 7 and §9,
engine/tests/{own_writes,hooks}.rs (the `the_lock_covers_the_write`
test and its fresh-file case).

## Outputs

The lock around the theme-hook install; a test that fails on a
lock-after-write mutant (held lock → exit 4, files byte-identical, no
owned.json record); one sentence in SPEC-ENGINE §9; HANDOVER with the
mutant evidence.

## Acceptance

`just check` 0; the mutant proof in the handover.
